# Credits and staging statement

**This apartment is staged.** Every photo in `Photos/` is a free-licence stock photo (Wikimedia Commons; originals published under CC0 or CC BY-SA). The move-out photos are programmatic re-shots of the same base photo (small crop, rotation, colour shift, noise); stains, nail holes, scuffs and blur are added by `tools/make_fixture.py`; EXIF dates are assigned, not real. The deduction letter and all names and addresses are fictional. Derived images are modified versions and are shared under the same licences as their sources.
