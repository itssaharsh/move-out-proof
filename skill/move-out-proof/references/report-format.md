# Report format

The report is a Markdown file the renter can attach to their reply to the landlord or to a deposit scheme. It states facts from their own library and leaves every judgement to them.

## Header

- Title: Move-out Proof report
- Letter: its date and sender, as extracted
- Deposit, total of proposed deductions, and balance offered, as extracted
- Response deadline, if the letter states one
- Windows searched: move-in window and move-out window, with dates

## Charge table

One row per charge, in the letter's order:

| Charge (as written) | Amount | Spot | Move-in photo | Move-out photo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oven deep clean | £180.00 | kitchen-oven | IMG_20240312_100412.jpg, 12 Mar 2024 10:04. Visible: none visible | IMG_20261030_140812.jpg, 30 Oct 2026 14:08. Visible: none visible |
| Bathroom deep clean | £30.00 | bathroom-tub | No dated move-in photo of this spot | IMG_20261030_142233.jpg, 30 Oct 2026 14:22. Visible: none visible |

Each photo cell holds the file name, the capture date and time from the photo metadata, and the vision note on what is visible. A missing side holds the gap sentence instead. There is no column for a verdict.

## After the table

1. **Gaps:** charges whose spot has no dated move-in photo or no dated move-out photo.
2. **Unclear photos:** file names and dates of photos the vision step could not read, so the renter can check them by eye.
3. **Charges with no photo spot:** with their amounts.
4. **Counts:** photos searched in each window, labelled, unclear, left out.
5. **Closing line, always included:** "Photos and dates come from your own library and its photo metadata. This report does not say whether any charge is fair; you decide what to dispute."
