# Spot list

The vision step labels every photo with exactly one of these. A fixed list keeps labels comparable between move-in and move-out photos, so a pair is always the same spot photographed twice.

| Label | What it covers | Charges it usually answers |
|---|---|---|
| `kitchen-oven` | oven, hob, extractor hood | oven cleaning, hob damage |
| `kitchen-counter` | worktops, cupboard fronts | worktop burns or stains, cupboard damage |
| `kitchen-sink` | kitchen sink and tap | limescale, sink damage |
| `fridge` | fridge or freezer, inside or outside | fridge cleaning, freezer damage |
| `bathroom-tub` | bath or shower tray, shower screen, tiles around it | bathroom cleaning, mould, sealant |
| `bathroom-sink` | basin, tap, mirror, vanity unit | basin cleaning or damage |
| `toilet` | toilet and cistern | toilet cleaning or damage |
| `living-room-wall` | walls and paint in the living room | wall repair, repainting, nail holes, scuffs |
| `living-room-carpet` | carpet, rug area or floor in the living room | carpet cleaning, stains, burns, floor damage |
| `bedroom-wall` | walls and paint in a bedroom | wall repair, repainting |
| `bedroom-carpet` | carpet or floor in a bedroom | carpet cleaning, stains |
| `window` | windows, frames, sills, blinds, curtains | window cleaning, blind or curtain damage |
| `front-door` | entrance door, lock, hallway near the entrance | door damage, lock replacement |

Two more labels are not spots:

- `unclear`: the photo is too dark, too blurry or too close to tell. Unclear photos are listed in the report with their date, never paired.
- `not-a-spot`: the photo is not a part of the home (people, food, a street, a pet, packing boxes). These are counted and left out.

When a charge names a room or item that is not on this list, show it as "No photo spot" rather than stretching a label to fit.
